# Persona priority lists by buying-committee role

Recap AI sells into a sales-org buying committee. The titles below are priority-ordered
within each role. The waterfall in contact-resolver works down economic buyer first, then
champion, then influencer. The blocker role is not a prospecting target; it is listed so
the committee is legible and so outreach anticipates the objection.

## Economic buyers (own the budget, sign the deal)
1. VP Sales
2. CRO
3. Chief Revenue Officer

## Champions (feel the pain, drive the internal case)
1. Head of RevOps
2. Sales Operations Manager
3. Sales Enablement Lead

## Influencers (shape the decision, rarely sign)
1. VP Engineering (owns the CRM integration)
2. Head of Customer Success (post-sale motion depends on Recap data)

## Blockers (can stall the deal, not a prospecting target)
1. VP Legal
2. Chief Compliance Officer
