# Your buyer personas

This is a starter config. Every `TODO(setup):` marker is a field you fill in. A fresh clone
fails the preflight gate until they are replaced. Run `/setup` to populate it from your own
documents, or edit by hand. For a worked version, see
[examples/config/personas.md](../examples/config/personas.md), which is Recap AI, a
fictional example rather than a default.

Who you sell to, by role. The companies you target live in [icp.md](icp.md); the product,
objections, and discovery questions live in [offering.md](offering.md). Skills that need
objections or discovery combine a persona here with offering.md at runtime.

The buying committee is priority-ordered for outbound: economic buyer first, then champion,
then influencer. The blocker is not a prospecting target; it is listed so the committee is
complete and so outreach can anticipate the objection.

Rename these headings to your real roles. At least one persona must be marked (Required),
and it should be the economic buyer.
