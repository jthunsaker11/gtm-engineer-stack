# Motion: expansion

## Overview
Outreach to an existing customer at a growth or renewal moment. This is a customer-success and
account-management motion, not a new-logo motion. The relationship is already established, so the tone
is a check-in grounded in the customer's own usage, not a pitch.

## Trigger
An existing customer hits a milestone: a usage threshold, a renewal window opening, or a feature-adoption
trigger.

## Anchor style
Customer milestone reference: the usage number they crossed, the renewal date approaching, or the
feature they adopted. The anchor is the customer's own activity, which makes the outreach relevant by
default.

## Cadence
Milestone-based: a single message per milestone. A follow-up goes out only on a positive response.
Break-up rule: no sequence. One message per milestone, then stop unless the customer engages.

## Tier defaults
Not tier-driven. Expansion runs on the existing-customer base, which sits outside the new-logo tiers.
Tag an account as expansion regardless of the tier it carried during acquisition.

## Approval requirements
Auto-send. The relationship exists, the context is the customer's own milestone, and the risk is low,
so these do not need per-touch human review.

## Suppression rules
Bypass most cold-outbound rules, because the customer relationship is the context and the frequency cap
built for cold prospects does not apply. Respect explicit opt-outs.

## Success signals
Expansion opportunity created, net revenue retention, upsell and cross-sell conversion, and renewal
rate. These are account-value metrics, not reply-rate metrics.
