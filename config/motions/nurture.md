# Motion: nurture

## Overview
Low-frequency, value-led touches that keep a fit account warm until the timing improves. Nurture is
not a closing motion; it is a staying-present motion. The goal is to be the name the account already
knows when a real trigger finally arrives, so a later signal-based or inbound touch lands warm.

## Trigger
Tier C accounts, or accounts that recently exited another motion (cold-outbound, signal-based) without
a response. Nurture is the catch-basin for fit-but-not-now accounts.

## Anchor style
Value-add content: a case study, an industry report, or an opinion piece. Most touches carry no ask;
the content is the value. This keeps the relationship alive without the fatigue of a repeated pitch.

## Cadence
Monthly, indefinite until reply or unsubscribe. One touch roughly every 30 days.
Break-up rule: none. Nurture continues until the account replies, unsubscribes, or a fresher motion
(signal-based, inbound-followup) takes over and pulls the account out.

## Tier defaults
The sole default for a Tier C account with zero fresh signals: no fit urgency and no live event, so
stay present without an ask. A Tier C account that does have a live signal goes to cold-outbound
instead. Nurture is also the destination when a Tier A or B account exhausts a cold-outbound or
signal-based sequence with no reply.

## Approval requirements
Auto-send. The frequency is low and the content is not a direct ask, so the risk per touch is low and
a person does not need to review each one.

## Suppression rules
Strict. Respect every suppression list: unsubscribe, bounce, do-not-contact, and the frequency cap.

## Success signals
Re-engagement rate (nurtured accounts that later reply or graduate to a higher-intent motion), content
engagement (opens and clicks), and eventual meeting rate. Measured over a long horizon, because the
payoff is timing, not immediacy.
