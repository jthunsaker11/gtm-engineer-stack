# Motion: wake-the-dead

## Overview
A single, honest re-engagement of a closed-lost account when something has genuinely changed. This is
not a re-sequence and not a pretend-nothing-happened restart. It is one message that acknowledges the
gap and the new context, sent only when there is a real reason to reopen the conversation.

## Trigger
A closed-lost account gains a new hire, goes through a reorg, or throws a fresh buying signal. The
change is what justifies the re-engagement; a closed-lost account with no change stays closed.

## Anchor style
Acknowledge the time that has passed and the new context (the new hire, the reorg, or the fresh signal).
Do not pretend the loss did not happen; the honesty is what makes the re-open credible.

## Cadence
A single re-engagement message. No follow-up unless the account responds.
Break-up rule: one and done. If there is no reply, return the account to closed-lost and wait for the
next genuine change.

## Tier defaults
Not tier-driven. Enrollment comes from the closed-lost list plus a fresh change, not from a new tier.

## Approval requirements
Human-approve. Closed-lost history needs judgment, because a clumsy re-open can damage a relationship
that might otherwise recover on its own.

## Suppression rules
Strict, plus an explicit closed-lost-cooldown check: no re-engagement within 6 months of the closed-lost
date. Respect every standard suppression list on top of the cooldown.

## Success signals
Re-open rate (closed-lost accounts that return to an active opportunity), reply rate, and meeting rate.
Volume is low and the value per win is high, so this motion is judged on quality of re-opens, not
throughput.
